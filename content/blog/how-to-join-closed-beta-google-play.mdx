Lumi for Android is on Google Play. You don't need an invite or a tester list: open the store page and tap **Install**.

## Install Lumi from Google Play

1. **Open the store page:** [Lumi on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.andrey5454germanov.moneytracker).
2. **Tap Install.** The app downloads and installs like any other.
3. **Open Lumi** and start logging expenses. No bank login is needed.

If the link opens the store but the app doesn't show, search for "Lumi: Budget & Expense Tracker" in the Play Store.

## Check you have the public app

Lumi on Google Play is the public release, so it updates automatically through the store. If you installed an older test build from an invite, uninstall it and install the app from the store page above.

## Common questions

### Is Lumi on Android free?

Lumi has a free plan. Premium features are available as an in-app subscription.

### Does Lumi on Android connect to my bank?

No. Lumi doesn't ask for a bank login. Entries are added manually, with voice input or receipt scanning.

### Which features differ from iPhone?

Back Tap and Apple Pay auto-import are iPhone features, so they aren't part of the Android version. Voice input, receipt scanning, home-screen widgets, budgets, savings goals and the month-end forecast work on both.
