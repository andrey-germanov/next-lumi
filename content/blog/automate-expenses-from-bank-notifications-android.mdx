Your bank already tells you about every card purchase. The notification pops up, you glance at it, and then the transaction is gone from your head. The purchase still has to be tracked somewhere, though, and typing it in by hand is the step most people quietly stop doing.

Lumi on Android turns those bank notifications into expenses. When a purchase notification arrives, Lumi reads the merchant and the amount and logs the expense for you, with a category picked from your history.

## Why bank notifications beat manual entry

Manual entry fails for a simple reason: it asks for effort at the moment you're least likely to want to give it. A notification solves that. The purchase is already happening, the bank has already written down the details, and the only job left is to save them.

There's a second benefit. Notifications come from the bank, so you don't have to share a bank login with a budgeting app. Lumi never asks for bank credentials. It only works with what your phone already receives.

## What the setup looks like

1. **Install Lumi from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.andrey5454germanov.moneytracker).**
2. **Give Lumi access to bank notifications** when the app asks.
3. **Add your banks.** Add each bank and card you use. Lumi doesn't limit how many you can connect.
4. **Pay as usual.** Each purchase notification becomes an expense with the merchant and amount.

After that there's nothing to maintain. You check your month, not your receipts.

## What gets logged, and what doesn't

Lumi logs the purchases your bank reports. If you want to adjust a category, that takes one tap.

## Nothing gets lost

The most common reason people abandon an expense tracker is a gap: a week of purchases they never entered, and then a month that doesn't add up. Automatic logging closes the gap. A notification that arrives while Lumi is closed is still picked up as an expense, so your month stays complete.

## Who this is for

Anyone who carries more than one card, has more than one bank, or simply doesn't want to type every coffee. It is also a good fit if you don't want to hand your bank login to an app, because the automation runs on notifications your phone already gets.

If you want the fastest way to start, install Lumi from Google Play, add your banks, and make one purchase. You'll see it appear within seconds.
