Most expense apps on Android connect to your bank through a login. You enter your online-banking credentials, the app pulls your transactions, and you hope the company keeps them safe. Plenty of people are uneasy about that, and they're right to think about it.

There's another way to get the same data without handing over a login: the notifications your bank already sends you when you pay.

## How notification-based tracking works

When you make a card purchase, your bank typically sends a push notification with the merchant and the amount. Lumi on Android reads those notifications and turns each purchase into an expense. Nothing is pulled from your bank account, and there's no separate connection to maintain.

The result is the same list of expenses you'd get from a bank connection, with two differences. First, your banking credentials never leave your bank. Second, the data stays on your device, which is where Lumi keeps it.

## Compared with a bank login

| | Bank login | Bank notifications (Lumi) |
|---|---|---|
| Shares your online-banking login | Yes | No |
| Works with banks in any country | Depends on the provider | Yes, any bank you add |
| Breaks when the bank changes its system | Sometimes | Rarely |
| Data stored on your device | Often not | Yes |

Bank connections can be convenient, and some people are happy with them. If you're not, notification-based tracking gives you automatic logging without the login.

## Setting it up on Android

Install Lumi from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.andrey5454germanov.moneytracker). Grant notification access when the app asks, add the banks and cards you use, and then make a purchase. The expense appears with the merchant, amount and a suggested category.

## What to check before you rely on it

- **Your bank must send a purchase notification.** Most do, but check that yours does for each card.
- **Notification access is a sensitive permission.** Only grant it to apps you trust.
- **Review the first month.** Spot-check a few purchases to confirm the amounts and merchants look right.

## The bottom line

If you want expense tracking on Android without a bank login, notification-based logging is the practical option. You get automatic entries, you keep your banking credentials to yourself, and nothing needs to be typed.
