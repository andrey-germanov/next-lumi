Plenty of people don't have one bank. There's the main account, a second card for travel, a savings app, perhaps a card from an old job or an account abroad. Each one shows its own transactions, in its own app, with its own format. Adding it up at the end of the month becomes a chore nobody finishes.

The fix is to pull every card into one list. On Android, Lumi does that from bank notifications.

## Why multiple banks break most trackers

Bank-login apps often support a limited number of connections, or only the banks they have an agreement with. If you use a bank they don't cover, that card simply doesn't appear. The gap is easy to miss until a month doesn't add up.

Notification-based logging doesn't have that limit in the same way. Each bank and card that sends a purchase notification can be added, and Lumi doesn't cap how many you connect.

## Setting up several banks

1. **Install Lumi from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.andrey5454germanov.moneytracker).**
2. **Grant notification access** when Lumi asks.
3. **Add every bank and card you use.** Add a bank from any country you spend in.
4. **Make a purchase with each card** once, to confirm it logs correctly.

## One list, one budget

With everything in one place, your budgets and forecast cover all of your spending, not just the card you happened to check. A purchase on your travel card and a coffee on your everyday card land in the same categories, so you can see where the money goes across your whole financial life.

Lumi also shows balances across all your currencies, which matters if one of your cards is in a different currency.

## Keep the list honest

- **Check that each card's purchases appear.** Do it for the first week, then occasionally.
- **Watch for duplicates** if you pay through a wallet app that also sends its own notification. Check the first few entries.
- **Fix categories once** when they're wrong, so the list stays easy to read.

## The result

A month where every card is in the same list, every purchase is logged, and nothing needs copying from one app to another. That is the point of tracking across banks: you stop guessing and start seeing the full picture.
